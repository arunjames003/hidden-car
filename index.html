Picture Reveal Game

HIDDEN CAR

A tiny piece of a car. Can you name it?

Your name and score appear on the worldwide leaderboard.

How to play

  1. A car photo is hidden behind tiles. One tile is open.
  2. Pick the car from 4 options. Open more tiles for clues.
  3. The fewer tiles you open, the more points you win.
  4. You have 2 lives (♥♥). A wrong answer costs a life and 100 points.
  5. 50/50 removes two wrong options. Once per game, costs 25% of that round.
